## Verified Organization Positioning

- Kids With Solutions Foundation, Ltd. (KWSF) is a Kenyan social enterprise.
- KWSF was founded in 2018 and registered in 2019 as a company limited by guarantee under the Companies Act, 2015.
- Its mission is to equip young people with skills and tools to thrive in the 21st century.
- Its supported learning areas include digital literacy, financial literacy, coding, robotics, leadership development, and innovation.
- Its design-thinking approach is presented as `Discover`, `Imagine`, `Create`, and `Evolve`.
- Its focus includes young people who face barriers to quality technology and financial literacy education.

## Generated Image Prompts

All replacement visuals used the built-in image generation tool. Each prompt specified a polished modern editorial illustration style, a warm African social-enterprise context, deep blue / muted green / warm amber / cream colors, no realistic identifiable faces, no logos, no readable text, no poverty stereotypes, no pity-based visuals, and no watermark.

1. **Learning-hub hero:** Young African learners shown from the back using laptops and a robotics kit in a safe community learning hub with a supportive mentor and design-thinking materials.
2. **Digital literacy:** Two young learners shown from behind using a laptop with simple folder, keyboard-practice, and safe-browsing icons with a mentor in side silhouette.
3. **Coding & robotics:** Three learners collaborating around a wheeled robot kit and a laptop with abstract block-code shapes while a mentor guides the project.
4. **Financial literacy:** Young learners using a laptop, calculator, notebook, and abstract budgeting icons with a supportive mentor nearby.
5. **Leadership & innovation:** Learners arranging sticky notes, sketching ideas, and testing a prototype using a `Discover`, `Imagine`, `Create`, `Evolve` style process.
6. **Enrollment:** A parent or guardian and learner shown from behind speaking with a program coordinator at a welcoming learning-hub consultation table.
7. **Contact & partnerships:** Two adults discussing a learning partnership with a laptop, notebook, communication icons, and a distant privacy-safe classroom scene.
8. **Support & access:** Staff reviewing laptops, charging equipment, books, and robotics kits prepared for responsible program delivery.

## Missing Information Needed Before Launch

- Official public email address, phone number, office or service location, and social links.
- Approved M-Pesa, bank, or online giving instructions and an official donor-enquiry contact.
- Current program age bands, schedules, venues, delivery formats, fees, and sponsored-place criteria.
- Approved learner safeguarding policy, safeguarding point of contact, and website link.
- Approved privacy notice covering learner and guardian data storage, use, sharing, retention, and removal.
- Verified impact metrics with reporting periods and methodology.
- Consent-approved testimonials or success stories.
- Current partner list and logo-publication approvals.
- Current board composition, exact name spellings, titles, biographies, and consented portraits.
